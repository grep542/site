Process

Four steps from portfolio
scan to filed credits

No manual research. No complex forms. Your firm stays in the advisory seat while Greptiles handles identification, calculation, and documentation.

Step 01

Upload your client list

Import a CSV or connect QuickBooks, Gusto, or Rippling. Greptiles ingests payroll, headcount, industry codes, and revenue data across your entire book of business.

Step 02

AI scans for eligibility

Our engine cross-references every client against IRS credit criteria and 35+ state incentive databases — R&D, ERC, WOTC, energy, startup payroll offsets — in minutes, not months.

Step 03

Review and approve

Your firm receives a prioritized list of eligible clients with estimated recovery amounts, credit types, and confidence scores. You decide which to pursue and when.

Step 04

We prepare, you file

Greptiles generates IRS-ready documentation, supporting calculations, and audit defense packages. Your CPAs review, sign off, and submit. You remain the advisor of record.

Greptiles covers the full landscape of federal and state tax incentives — not just the obvious ones. Most clients qualify for multiple credits they've never claimed.

ERC Refund
Up to $26K
Cash refund Per employee
Clients with W-2 employees in 2020–2021 who experienced revenue decline or operational disruption. Billions remain unclaimed — most firms have missed this for their clients.
R&D Tax Credit
$30K–$80K
Annual All industries
Applies to any client who develops, improves, or customizes software, products, or processes. The IRS definition is substantially broader than most CPAs apply in practice.
State R&D Credits
$5K–$20K
Stackable 35+ states
Most clients claim either the federal credit or their state credit — rarely both. Greptiles files them simultaneously, stacking recoveries that would otherwise be left unclaimed.
Work Opportunity
Up to $9,600
Per qualifying hire Retroactive
WOTC credits for clients who hire veterans, long-term unemployed, formerly incarcerated individuals, and other target groups. Consistently overlooked across most client portfolios.
Startup Payroll Offset
Up to $250K/yr
Pre-revenue eligible 5 years
Startups under five years old with under $5M revenue can apply R&D credits against payroll tax obligations — even before profitability. Almost universally unfiled across most practices.
Energy Credits
Up to $50K
IRA expanded Commercial property
The Inflation Reduction Act dramatically expanded commercial energy credits for solar, HVAC, insulation, and EVs. Applies to clients in owned or leased commercial space — regardless of industry.
