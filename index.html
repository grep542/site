FAQ
Questions we get asked
Do I need to install anything?
No. You connect your Shopify store via OAuth (one click), and link WhatsApp by scanning a QR code. That's it. No app to download, no code to write.
Is my store data safe?
Yes. We only read the data we need (orders, inventory, customers). We never write to your store without your explicit instruction. Data is encrypted and never shared or sold.
What Shopify plan do I need?
Any Shopify plan works — Basic, Shopify, Advanced, or Plus. We use the standard Shopify API which is available on all plans.
Can I change when the briefing arrives?
On the Growth and Pro plans, yes — you can set any time you want. Starter defaults to 9:00 AM IST.
What language does the briefing come in?
English by default. Hindi support is coming soon. The numbers are always in INR for Indian stores, or your store's currency.
How is this different from Shopify's built-in analytics?
Shopify analytics are a dashboard — you have to open it, find what you need, and interpret it. StoreWhisper pushes the insight to you in plain English, flags what matters, and can take action. You never need to open a dashboard again.
